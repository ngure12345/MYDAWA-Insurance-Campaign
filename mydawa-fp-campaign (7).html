Stop paying out of pocket for what your insurance covers

The National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF) and many private health schemes in Kenya cover a range of family planning services. Most women don't know — and end up paying unnecessarily. Here's how to use your cover on MYDAWA.

1
Check your policy benefits Log into your NHIF or private insurer portal, or call your insurer directly to confirm family planning coverage.
2
Book a clinical consultation on MYDAWA Our trained healthcare providers will advise on the right method and confirm your insurance eligibility.
3
Shop or visit a partner clinic Purchase products on MYDAWA or get procedures done at an insurer-accredited facility at zero or minimal cost.

FAQs

Questions women ask. Honest answers.

Does my NHIF cover family planning?+
NHIF covers many family planning services at accredited facilities, including IUD insertion, sterilisation procedures, injectables, and clinical consultations. Coverage depends on your contribution level and the facility. Book a MYDAWA consultation and our clinicians will confirm your specific entitlements before your visit.
What about private insurance coverage?+
Many private health schemes (AAR, Jubilee, CIC, GA Insurance, etc.) include a maternity or wellness benefit that covers contraceptives, consultations, and clinical procedures. Coverage varies significantly by plan. Check your policy document or call your insurer's customer service to confirm.
Which method is best if I want children in 1–2 years?+
Short-acting reversible methods are best: the combined pill, mini-pill, condoms, or injectables. Fertility returns quickly after stopping. Avoid Depo-Provera if you plan to conceive soon as it can delay fertility return by 6–18 months.
Can I use family planning while breastfeeding?+
Yes. Safe options while breastfeeding include: the progestogen-only pill, copper IUD, implant, condoms, and LAM (if you meet all 3 criteria). Avoid combined pills in the first 6 months as estrogen may reduce milk supply.
Does family planning cause permanent infertility?+
No. All modern hormonal methods (except sterilisation) are fully reversible. Fertility returns immediately after stopping pills, or after removing an implant or IUD. Depo-Provera may delay return by 6–18 months.
Which method also protects against STIs?+
Only male and female condoms protect against both STIs and pregnancy. Using condoms alongside another method like the pill or implant is called “dual protection” — the gold standard for comprehensive reproductive health.
Can I get family planning privately, without my partner knowing?+
Yes. You have the legal right to access family planning privately. MYDAWA offers discreet delivery and private online consultations. Your health decisions belong to you.
How does emergency contraception work?+
Postinor-2 and Lydia work by delaying or preventing ovulation. They do NOT terminate an existing pregnancy. Most effective within 24 hours (89%) but can be taken up to 72–120 hours after unprotected sex. No prescription needed — order on MYDAWA.
